# Slides

`JABS_module_companion_deck.pptx` is the companion deck for the module: the concepts behind the
live demo (labeling, features and windows, evaluation, bouts, and the biology), with speaker notes.

Figures come from Geuther et al. 2021 (eLife), Choudhary et al. 2026 (eLife) and Ye et al. 2024
(Nature Communications), all CC BY 4.0, and are credited on their slides. The example grooming clips
use the Zenodo JABS example projects (doi:10.5281/zenodo.16697331). The References slide lists the sources; the backup slides after it hold extra material.
